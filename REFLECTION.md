# Reflection

Do not submit generic answers here. The PDF requires 150-400 words for each answer, and reviewers check whether the details match the git history and code.

## 1. The hardest bug you hit this week, and how you debugged it

Draft after the bug is real. Include the symptom, the first wrong hypothesis, the logs or reproduction steps, what changed your mind, and the final fix. A credible answer should name exact files, commands, and code paths.

## 2. A decision you reversed mid-week, and what made you reverse it

Draft after you actually reverse a decision. Good candidates: client-only save flow versus server route, Supabase versus Firebase, hCaptcha versus honeypot, or AI-generated recommendations versus deterministic audit rules.

## 3. What you would build in week 2 if you had it

Draft after the MVP works. The strongest answer should prioritize by business impact: benchmark mode, renewal monitoring, PDF export, referral loop, admin triage, or invoice import.

## 4. How you used AI tools

Draft honestly. Name the tools used, what you trusted them for, what you did not trust them for, and one specific time an AI suggestion was wrong and you caught it. For this project, do not claim AI calculated pricing or savings.

## 5. Self-rating

Use this exact structure in the final version:

| Dimension | Rating | Reason |

| Code quality | X/10 | One sentence tied to types, tests, and maintainability. |

| Problem-solving | X/10 | One sentence tied to debugging and tradeoffs. |
